The Fellowship

7-month eAIrth Fellowship (with stipend) · January 1 – July 31, 2027

Six participants who are residents of the City of Richmond, Henrico or Chesterfield will receive $1,000.

What You'll Do

The $1,000 is for:

  • participation in eight preparatory meetings of 2 hours each; and
  • producing a "Signature Piece" (sculpture, dance, song, video, photography, poetry, essay, Tik-Tok, etc.) to share at two community events with policymakers and on-line in a virtual display.
